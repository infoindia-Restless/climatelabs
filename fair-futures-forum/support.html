Fair Futures Forum / How we can scale it up

What it will take

Realising this vision for Bengaluru and beyond needs sustained, flexible support across three dimensions.

Long-term flexible funding

Core funding
A multi-year commitment for coordinating collaborative activity, implementing community-based climate initiatives, and building and maintaining shared knowledge assets.
Project-specific funding
Dedicated multi-year support for initiatives within the five outcome areas, such as ward vulnerability assessments, the citizens' jury and pilot demonstrations.

A strategic communication platform

Digital infrastructure
A platform for knowledge products and resources, community stories and case studies, and visualisations of climate vulnerability and action data.
Outreach and engagement
Support for documenting FFF initiatives and impact, media engagement, public events and translation of outputs.

Technical and network support

Capacity building
Training for partners in climate science and communication, policy advocacy, participatory research methods, and monitoring and evaluation.
Network expansion
Bringing in more civil society organisations, academic institutions, private sector partners and government departments beyond environmental agencies.

Interested in funding or joining the Fair Futures Forum? Talk to any of our partners, or start with Restless Development.
