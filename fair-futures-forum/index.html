Climate action cannot work without people.

The Fair Futures Forum is a collective of organisations working towards climate action that is fair, inclusive and people-centred. By bringing together communities, civil society and practitioners, it connects everyday realities with city-level climate planning and action in Bengaluru.

We turn data, research and community knowledge into stories and shared understanding, so that communities, young people and practitioners can recognise the urgency of urban climate issues and help shape fair solutions.
