Climate Kaṭṭe

A podcast by Restless Development India, named after the village gathering place under the peepal tree.

The podcast

A community space for conversations on climate and how it connects with our lives.

Climate Kaṭṭe takes its name from the AraLi Kaṭṭe, the raised platform under the peepal tree where people once gathered to talk, share news and settle matters together.

In the same spirit, Restless Development India hosts inclusive conversations with people of diverse expertise and viewpoints, who share their journeys and what climate change means in their work and lives.
